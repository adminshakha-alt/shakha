Delete your Shakha 360 account

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This page explains how a firm deletes its data in Shakha 360, how to close a firm's account or one person's login, and what is deleted. It applies to the website and to the Shakha 360 Android app. In the app, open Help → Policies → Delete my account to reach this page.

1. Delete a firm's data yourself

A firm can delete its own data at any time, without writing to us:

  1. The head office admin opens Settings → Logins & Backup → Reset all data, types DELETE and enters the code sent to the head office email.
  2. If the firm has branches, every branch is asked, on screen and by email, to approve or decline. Nothing is deleted until every branch approves. If any branch declines, the request closes and nothing is deleted. Head office can cancel the request while it waits.
  3. Once every branch has approved (or at once, for a firm with no branches), Shakha 360 saves a full backup in the firm's Google Drive and then deletes, for head office and every branch: invoices, proformas, receipts, credit and debit notes, recurring invoices, client work and filings, document requests, notes on clients, notices and appeals, the Client Files list and its share links, and Client Logins. The party list is deleted too, unless head office chose to keep it.

Everyone involved is told of each step. Settings, branches, billing firms, logins, and certificates and letters stay, so the firm can start again. Files already saved in the firm's Drive (PDFs, client documents and backups) stay there; the firm can delete them from its Drive. To close the account as well, see section 2.

2. Close a firm's account

The firm's head office admin emails privacy@shakha360.com from the admin email address registered with Shakha 360, with the subject "Delete my firm's Shakha 360 account" and the firm's name. We may reply to confirm the request before deleting.

Within 30 days we delete:

  • the firm's registration;
  • all of the firm's logins, with their passwords, profiles (mobile number and date of birth) and signed-in devices;
  • the firm's Google permission tokens, after cancelling them with Google;
  • the firm's Tally Connector connections, which stop working with the registration. Uninstall the program from the firm's computers to remove it completely.

The firm's own data is kept in the firm's own Google Drive (the folder "Shakha – firm name"). It belongs to the firm and stays there; the firm can delete that folder from its Drive at any time. Before asking for deletion, a firm can back up everything from Settings → Logins & Backup, and can delete its data first as in section 1.

3. Delete one person's login

Logins belong to the firm that added them, so the firm's head office or branch admin removes a login from the logins list in Shakha 360's Settings. A person whose login is removed is signed out at once.

A person can also email privacy@shakha360.com from their login's email address. Within 30 days we delete their password, profile (mobile number and date of birth) and signed-in devices, and we tell their firm so that it can remove the login. The invoices, filings and other work they did stay in the firm's records, because those records belong to the firm.

4. The Android app

Uninstalling the Shakha 360 app deletes everything the app kept on the phone: the sign-in, the daily summary and the app's settings. It does not delete the firm's data or the account; use sections 1 to 3 for that. The same holds for the Login Filler browser extension and the Tally Connector program: removing them from a computer removes what they kept there.

5. Questions

Write to privacy@shakha360.com. Our Privacy Policy explains what Shakha 360 keeps and why.
