Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Shakha ("we") provides billing and compliance software to chartered accountant firms ("firms"). This policy explains what information Shakha uses, why, and how firms control it.

1. Information we use

  • Account information: the name and Gmail address of each person a firm adds as a login, and the firm's registration details (firm name, city, GSTIN, phone, number of branches).
  • Firm records: the data a firm enters in Shakha, such as clients, invoices, receipts, compliance work and settings.
  • Sign-in and activity: one-time sign-in codes (kept for 10 minutes, stored only as a secure hash), sign-in times and an activity log of changes made in the firm's Shakha.

2. How Shakha uses your Google account

When a firm's head office admin connects a Google account, Shakha asks Google for these permissions only:

  • See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files that you use with this app (drive.file): to create the folder "Shakha – firm name" and the firm's data sheet in the firm's own Drive, and to save the firm's invoice PDFs, statements and backups there. Shakha cannot see or open any other file in your Drive.
  • Send email on your behalf (gmail.send): to send the emails the firm uses Shakha for, from the firm's Gmail: sign-in codes for its staff, approval notices to its branches, invoices, payment and due-date reminders to its clients. Shakha cannot read, search or delete your email.
  • Your email address (openid, email): to confirm that the Google account connected is the firm's registered head office admin.

To run the app, the firm's Shakha folder is shared with the Shakha service account. Shakha stores a permission token for the connected account, used only to perform the actions above.

3. Limited use of Google user data

Shakha's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, we use this data only to provide Shakha's features to the firm; we do not sell it, use it for advertising, use it to train any AI or machine-learning model, or let anyone read it except (a) with the firm's permission, for example to give support it asks for, (b) for security or to comply with the law, or (c) in automated processing needed to run the app.

4. Where data is kept

A connected firm's data sheet and files are kept in the firm's own Google Drive and owned by the firm. The list of registered firms and logins is kept in Shakha's own Google account. Google's own privacy policy applies to data stored with Google.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not share it with third parties except Google, which hosts and runs the service, or where the law requires.

6. Your choices and deletion

  • A firm can export its data at any time (Settings → Logins & Backup).
  • A firm can remove Shakha's access at any time from its Google Account → Security → Third-party connections. Its files stay in its Drive.
  • To stop using Shakha and have its registration and logins deleted, a firm emails us at the address below; we delete them within 30 days. The firm's own Drive files remain the firm's.

7. Security

Access is by one-time codes sent to registered Gmail addresses; sessions expire automatically; each firm's data is kept separate from every other firm's. No method is perfectly secure, and firms should keep their Google accounts protected with 2-step verification.

8. Children

Shakha is for businesses and is not meant for children.

9. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: admin.shakha@gmail.com
