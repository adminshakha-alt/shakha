Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Shakha 360 ("we") provides billing and compliance software to chartered accountant firms ("firms"). This policy explains what information Shakha 360 uses, why, and how firms and the people who sign in control it.

1. Information we use

  • Logins: the name and email address of each person a firm adds as a login, the office and access level the firm gives them, and the time they last signed in. A person may also add a mobile number and date of birth to their profile; the date of birth is used only for a birthday greeting, which the firm can switch off.
  • Registration: the firm's name, city, state, GSTIN, phone, number of branches, the plan chosen and the admin's email address.
  • Firm records: what a firm enters in Shakha 360, such as clients and their contact details, invoices, receipts, compliance work, filings, certificates and letters, and notes on clients.
  • Client documents: files a firm's clients upload through a private link the firm sends them (PDF, JPG, PNG, Excel, Word and CSV files only).
  • Acceptance of engagement letters: when a firm sends a client its engagement letter for acceptance, the name and designation the client types, the email address the one-time code was sent to, the time of acceptance, a description of the browser used and a fingerprint (SHA-256) of the letter. These are kept with the letter as the firm's record of the agreement and printed on it.
  • Sign-in details: passwords, kept only as a salted hash that cannot be turned back into the password; one-time sign-in codes, kept for 10 minutes as a hash; and, for each device signed in, a short description of the device and the times it was used.
  • Robot check (Google reCAPTCHA): on the registration page, on Forgot password, and on sign-in after a wrong password or code for that email address in the last hour, Shakha 360 asks the person to tick "I'm not a robot". This check is Google reCAPTCHA, which Google loads on the page only at that step. reCAPTCHA works by collecting hardware and software information, such as device and browser data, and sending it to Google for analysis, to tell people apart from automated programs. Shakha 360 sends Google only the result of the check to confirm it, and does not keep it. Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply to reCAPTCHA.
  • Activity: a log of changes made in the firm's Shakha 360, showing who did what. It is deleted after 7 days.

2. How Shakha 360 uses your Google account

When a firm's head office admin connects a Google account, Shakha 360 asks Google for these permissions only:

  • See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files that you use with this app (drive.file): to create the folder "Shakha – firm name" and the firm's data sheet in the firm's own Drive, and to save the firm's invoice PDFs, statements, certificates, letters, client documents and backups there. Shakha 360 cannot see or open any other file in your Drive.
  • Send email on your behalf (gmail.send): to send the emails the firm uses Shakha 360 for, from the firm's Gmail: sign-in codes and access emails for its people, approval notices to its branches, invoices, payment and due-date reminders, document requests and greetings to its clients. Shakha 360 cannot read, search or delete your email.
  • Your email address (openid, email): to confirm that the Google account connected is the firm's registered head office admin.

A branch of a firm can connect its own Google account in the same way, with the same permissions. That branch's PDFs and the documents its clients upload are then kept in its own Drive, and its emails go from its own Gmail, while its records stay in the firm's data sheet.

To run the app, the firm's Shakha 360 folder is shared with Shakha 360's own Google account (admin.shakha@gmail.com), which the service uses to keep the firm's data sheet up to date. Shakha 360 stores a permission token for each connected account, used only to perform the actions above. A firm's other logins, including staff logins, are given no access to the firm's Google account.

3. Limited use of Google user data

Shakha 360's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, we use this data only to provide Shakha 360's features to the firm; we do not sell it or pass it to data brokers, use it for advertising of any kind, use it to decide credit-worthiness or for lending, use it to train any AI or machine-learning model, or let anyone read it except (a) with the firm's permission, for example to give support it asks for, (b) for security or to comply with the law, or (c) in automated processing needed to run the app. Shakha 360 does not use Google Workspace APIs to develop, improve, or train non-personalized AI and/or ML models, and does not transfer Google user data to anyone else.

How long Google user data is kept. Shakha 360 keeps the connected Google account's email address and its permission token for as long as the firm uses Shakha 360. When the firm's registration is deleted, the token is cancelled with Google and deleted together with the email address. A firm can also remove Shakha 360's access at any time in its Google Account, which stops all access at once. The folder, data sheet and files Shakha 360 created in the firm's Drive belong to the firm and stay there until the firm deletes them.

4. Where data is kept, and who sees it

A connected firm's data sheet and files are kept in the firm's own Google Drive and owned by the firm. The list of registered firms and their logins, passwords (as hashes) and sign-in sessions is kept in Shakha 360's own Google account. Google's own privacy policy applies to data stored with Google.

Within a firm, head office logins see the firm's records. A branch login sees its own branch's records. A staff login receives only the clients and work its office allots to it, and only what its office allows, such as client contact details or fees. Notes a firm keeps on a client are seen only by the office that wrote them, and never by the client. Documents a client uploads are kept in the connected Drive of the office that asked for them, in the folder Client Documents, and are shown only to that office's logins.

The Shakha 360 administrator sees, for each firm, its registration details, counts of its records, and the name, email address, access level and last sign-in of each login, so as to run the service and apply the firm's plan. The administrator never sees a firm's records, documents, notes or passwords.

5. Clients of a firm

A firm enters its clients' names and contact details to bill them and remind them. Shakha 360 sends emails to clients only when the firm, or a reminder the firm has set up, asks it to, and always from the firm's or branch's own email account. Messages on WhatsApp open in the firm's own WhatsApp; Shakha 360 does not send them.

A document request gives the client a private link. No sign-in is needed to use it, and anyone with the link can upload to that request until it closes, 15 days after its due date. The client can mark a document as not applicable, and sees only their own request, never the firm's records.

An engagement letter sent for acceptance also gives the client a private link, which shows that letter alone. Accepting it needs a 6-digit code emailed to the address the firm has for the client; the code works for 10 minutes and for a limited number of tries, and is kept only as a hash. Once accepted, the letter cannot be changed in Shakha 360, and the client and the firm each receive a confirmation with the letter attached. The firm may withdraw a letter it has sent; the link then stops working.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not share it with third parties except Google, which hosts and runs the service and provides the reCAPTCHA robot check described in section 1, or where the law requires.

7. Your choices and deletion

  • A firm can back up all its data to its own Drive at any time (Settings → Logins & Backup).
  • A firm can remove Shakha 360's access at any time from its Google Account → Security → Third-party connections. Its files stay in its Drive.
  • A firm adds and removes its own logins. A person whose login is removed is signed out at once, and their name stays on the records of work they did.
  • A person can change their password, see which devices are signed in to their login, and sign the others out, from their profile in Shakha 360.
  • To stop using Shakha 360 and have its registration and logins deleted, a firm emails us at the address below; we delete them within 30 days. The firm's own Drive files remain the firm's.
  • When a firm's registration is deleted, Shakha 360 cancels its Google permission tokens with Google and deletes them, along with its logins' passwords and profiles.

8. Security

A person signs in with their email address and a password, or with a one-time code emailed to them. Passwords must be at least 8 characters with letters and numbers, and are never stored or sent in readable form. A code works for 10 minutes and for a limited number of tries. To stop automated attempts, registration, Forgot password and repeated sign-in tries also ask for Google reCAPTCHA's "I'm not a robot" check (section 1). Each device signed in has its own session, which ends when the person signs out, when their login is removed or changed, or after 30 days. A sign-in from a new device is reported to the person, and a phone can be signed in from a computer with a code that lasts a few minutes. Each firm's data is kept separate from every other firm's. All data travels between the browser, Shakha 360 and Google over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Google permission tokens are kept in Shakha 360's private server settings, are never shown to anyone, and are used only by the service to do what section 2 describes. No method is perfectly secure, and firms should keep their Google accounts protected with 2-step verification.

9. The Android app

There is no Shakha 360 app for Android to download yet; it is being built, and until then firms use the website in the phone’s browser. When it is released, the app will show the same screens as the website. It keeps the person's sign-in on the phone so that it can show a daily summary and a home-screen widget, and it can lock the app behind the phone's own screen lock. Nothing is sent anywhere other than Shakha 360.

10. Children

Shakha 360 is for businesses and is not meant for children.

11. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. If we change how Shakha 360 uses Google user data, we email each firm's head office admin before the change takes effect.
