Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Shakha 360 ("we") provides billing and compliance software to chartered accountant firms ("firms"). This policy explains what information Shakha 360 uses, why, and how firms and the people who sign in control it.

Our role. The records a firm keeps about its clients belong to the firm. Shakha 360 handles them on the firm's behalf and only to provide the service; the firm decides what it records and is responsible to its clients for it. For the people who sign in (logins) and for firms' registrations, Shakha 360 is itself responsible for the data described here.

1. Information we use

  • Logins: the name and email address of each person a firm adds as a login, their office and access level, and when they last signed in. A person may add a mobile number and date of birth to their profile; the date of birth is used only for a birthday greeting, which the firm can switch off.
  • Registration: the firm's name, city, state, GSTIN, phone, number of branches, plan and the admin's email address.
  • Firm records: what a firm enters, such as clients and their contact details, invoices, receipts, compliance work, notices, certificates, letters and notes; and its partners' names, membership numbers, DIN or DPIN, and the signature and seal images printed on its documents.
  • Client documents and answers: files a firm's clients upload through a private link the firm sends them, the answers they type to the firm's questions, and files the firm keeps in Client Files. So that the firm and its client can later show what was received, each file's name, size, time and fingerprint (SHA-256) are kept, along with earlier versions of an answer and a note when the firm removes a file.
  • Acceptance of engagement letters: the name and designation the client types, the email address the code was sent to, the time, a description of the browser and a fingerprint (SHA-256) of the letter, kept with the letter as the firm's record.
  • Sign-in details: passwords, kept only as a salted hash that cannot be turned back into the password; one-time codes, kept for 10 minutes as a hash; and a short description of each device signed in and when it was used.
  • Robot check (Google reCAPTCHA): registration, Forgot password and sign-in after a wrong try ask the person to tick "I'm not a robot". Google loads reCAPTCHA only at that step; it collects hardware and software information, such as device and browser data, and sends it to Google to tell people from automated programs. Shakha 360 does not keep the result. Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.
  • Activity: a log of who changed what, deleted after 7 days. The record of who viewed or changed a client's portal password is kept with that login (section 10).
  • Server logs: for each request, Google Cloud keeps the time, IP address, browser description and the action asked for. They hold no names, amounts or record contents, are used to keep the service running and secure, and are deleted after 30 days.
  • In the browser: sign-in and screen preferences are kept in the browser's own storage. There are no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

2. How Shakha 360 uses your Google account

When a firm's head office admin connects a Google account, Shakha 360 asks Google for these permissions only:

  • See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files that you use with this app (drive.file): to create the folder "Shakha – firm name" and the firm's data sheet in the firm's own Drive, and to save its invoice PDFs, statements, certificates, letters, client documents, receipts and backups there. Shakha 360 cannot see or open any other file in your Drive.
  • Send email on your behalf (gmail.send): to send the firm's emails from its Gmail, such as access emails for its people, notices to its branches, and invoices, reminders, document requests, receipts and greetings to its clients. Shakha 360 cannot read, search or delete your email.
  • Your email address (openid, email): to confirm that the account connected is the firm's registered head office admin.

A branch can connect its own Google account in the same way. Its PDFs and client documents are then kept in its own Drive and its emails go from its own Gmail, while its records stay in the firm's data sheet.

Emails that always come from Shakha 360. Sign-in codes, security alerts, a person's first access email, the codes to reset a firm's data or accept an engagement letter, and the backup files in section 7 are sent from Shakha 360's own addresses (hello@shakha360.com and alerts@shakha360.com) through Zoho Mail, on servers in India, so that no one else with access to the firm's mailbox can read them. A firm that has not connected a Google account has all its emails sent this way, with replies going to the firm.

To run the app, the firm's Shakha 360 folder is shared with Shakha 360's own Google account (admin.shakha@gmail.com), which keeps the data sheet up to date. Shakha 360 stores a permission token for each connected account, used only for the actions above. Staff and other logins get no access to the firm's Google account.

3. Limited use of Google user data

Shakha 360's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, we use this data only to provide Shakha 360's features to the firm; we do not sell it or pass it to data brokers, use it for advertising of any kind, use it to decide credit-worthiness or for lending, use it to train any AI or machine-learning model, or let anyone read it except (a) with the firm's permission, for example to give support it asks for, (b) for security or to comply with the law, or (c) in automated processing needed to run the app. Shakha 360 does not use Google Workspace APIs to develop, improve, or train non-personalized AI and/or ML models, and does not transfer Google user data to anyone else.

How long Google user data is kept. The connected account's email address and permission token are kept for as long as the firm uses Shakha 360. When the firm's registration is deleted, the token is cancelled with Google and deleted with the email address. A firm can remove Shakha 360's access at any time in its Google Account. The folder, data sheet and files in the firm's Drive belong to the firm and stay there until the firm deletes them.

4. Where data is kept, and who sees it

A connected firm's data sheet and files are kept in its own Google Drive. The list of registered firms, their logins, password hashes and sessions is kept in Shakha 360's own Google account. The server runs on Google Cloud in Mumbai, India; Google Drive and Sheets may store data in India or other countries, under Google's own privacy policy. Each branch's rows in the data sheet are sealed (encrypted) by the server, with the key kept in Shakha 360's private server settings and Google Secret Manager, never in the sheet.

Head office logins see the firm's records, a branch login sees its own branch's, and a staff login sees only the clients and work allotted to it, with what its office allows. Notes on a client are seen only by the office that wrote them. Documents a client uploads are shown only to the office that asked for them.

The Shakha 360 administrator sees each firm's registration, counts of its records, and each login's name, email address, access level and last sign-in, to run the service and apply the plan. The administrator never sees a firm's records, documents, notes or passwords.

5. Clients of a firm

Shakha 360 emails a firm's clients only when the firm, or a reminder it has set up, asks it to. When a client sends documents or answers, they can be emailed a receipt listing what was received, with a PDF copy kept in the firm's Client Files; the firm chooses this for each request. WhatsApp messages open in the firm's own WhatsApp; Shakha 360 does not send them.

Clients use private links that need no sign-in: a document request (anyone with the link can upload to it until it closes, 15 days after its due date), an engagement letter (accepted with a 6-digit code emailed to the client), and files the firm shares from Client Files (until the date the firm sets, with a record of when it was opened). A client sees only what that link is for, never the firm's records.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not share it with third parties except Google, which hosts and runs the service (Google Cloud, Google Drive, Google Sheets and Gmail) and provides the reCAPTCHA check in section 1; Zoho, which sends Shakha 360's own emails and holds mail sent to our addresses; or where the law requires.

7. Your choices and deletion

  • Each office can save a backup of its own records to its Drive at any time (My Account → Backup & Reset). A branch admin is also emailed the branch's backup before losing access (branch closed, login switched off, or registration deleted), and an associate office without its own Drive is emailed one every week.
  • A firm can remove Shakha 360's access from its Google Account → Security → Third-party connections. Its files stay in its Drive.
  • A firm can delete its data from My Account → Backup & Reset → Reset All Data, with a code sent to head office and the approval of every branch admin. Backups are saved first.
  • When an associate office closes or leaves, head office stops seeing its clients at once, and they are deleted 30 days later. Invoices, receipts and notes are kept, as GST law needs them.
  • A firm adds and removes its own logins. A removed person is signed out at once; their name stays on the work they did. A person can change their password and sign out other devices from their profile.
  • To stop using Shakha 360, a firm emails us and we delete its registration and logins within 30 days. A few items (the portal-password key, each branch's name, code and last backup date, and the sealing key copy) are kept 90 days more so that a branch can still start its own firm, then deleted. Delete your account explains the steps.
  • A person can ask, from their login's email address, for their own login details to be deleted. Within 30 days we delete their password, profile and devices and tell their firm. The work they did stays in the firm's records.
  • A person can ask what personal data we hold about them, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and name someone to act for them if they die or cannot act. Where the data is part of a firm's records, we pass the request to the firm, which decides on it.

8. Security

Passwords are never stored or sent in readable form, sign-in codes work for 10 minutes and a limited number of tries, and each device's session ends on sign-out, when the login changes, or after 30 days. A sign-in from a new device is reported to the person. Each firm's data is kept separate, branch rows are sealed (section 4), Google permission tokens are kept in private server settings, and all data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method is perfectly secure, and firms should protect their Google accounts with 2-step verification.

If we learn of a breach that affects a firm's data or a person's personal data, we tell those affected without delay, say what happened and what to do, and report it where Indian law requires, including to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. The Android app

The Android app shows the same screens as the website. On the phone it keeps the sign-in, used only with Shakha 360 to fetch a short daily summary for a notification and a home-screen widget, and its own settings (notification, lock and hour). The lock uses the phone's own screen lock, and nothing about it leaves the phone. Choosing a contact opens the phone's picker and fills in only that one entry; the app cannot read the contact list. "Add to calendar" opens the phone's calendar for the person to save; the app does not read the calendar. Downloads are saved to Downloads/Shakha. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it kept on the phone.

10. Client portal logins and the Login Filler

A firm can keep its clients' logins for government portals (such as Income Tax, GST, TRACES, MCA, EPFO, ESIC and the E-way bill portal) in Shakha 360's Client Logins page, only with the client's permission.

  • Storage: the password is encrypted before it is saved, with a key kept in Shakha 360's private server settings, never in the data sheet, downloads or backups in readable form.
  • Who sees them: each office sees only its own clients' logins, and a staff login only those of its allotted clients. Each time a password is shown, copied, used to sign in or changed, the person's name and the time are recorded for partners and admins to see; the last 100 are kept with the login.
  • The Login Filler browser extension (Chrome and Edge) is optional. When a person clicks Open and sign in, Shakha 360 passes that one login to the extension, which opens the portal in a new tab, keeps the user ID and password in the browser's memory for that tab only, for at most 4 minutes, and fills only those two boxes. It never types the captcha or OTP and never clicks Login. It has no server, stores nothing on the computer, sends nothing to us or anyone else, collects nothing about browsing, and works only on Shakha 360 and the portal pages listed on its store page.

11. The Tally Connector

The optional Tally Connector for Windows reads an office's invoices, notes, receipts and the clients they name, and sends them only to TallyPrime on that computer or the office's network. It reads no logos, signatures, passwords or client documents, and Tally's data is not sent to Shakha 360. It keeps a sign-in key encrypted by Windows, which can only send entries to Tally, and tells Shakha 360 only the computer's name, its version, the Tally company, and how many entries were sent or are waiting. An admin can switch it off in Settings → Tally; uninstalling removes it and its key.

12. Children

Shakha 360 is for businesses and is not meant for children.

13. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. If we change how Shakha 360 uses Google user data, we email each firm's head office admin before the change takes effect.

14. Contact and grievances

Questions, requests about your data, and complaints: privacy@shakha360.com. We acknowledge each request within 7 days and answer it within 30 days.
