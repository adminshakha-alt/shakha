Everything a CA firm bills, collects and files

Nine parts that work together. Open any of them to see the full list of what it does.

Billing

GST tax invoices, proformas, receipts with TDS, credit and debit notes, and recurring retainer bills.

All billing features
  • CGST and SGST or IGST worked out from the place of supply
  • Each office has its own numbering, GSTIN and bank details
  • GSTIN auto-fill: type a GSTIN and the party’s details fill in
  • Services list with SAC codes and default fees
  • Proforma invoices, converted to a tax invoice in one click
  • Receipts with TDS deducted, part payments and advances
  • Credit and debit notes, including the right way to change or cancel an invoice after its GST return is filed
  • Recurring invoices for retainers, with a “to bill” count
  • PDF, print, email from your own Gmail, WhatsApp and Drive links
  • Classic, Modern, Minimal and Traditional invoice templates, with your colours, fonts, logo, bank and UPI details, and the Udyam number if you want it
  • A saved signature, a DSC box or space to sign, chosen per invoice

Collections

See who owes what and remind them automatically, so fees don’t go uncollected.

All collection features
  • Outstanding and overdue lists, with follow-up notes
  • Automatic payment reminders to clients
  • Receivable ageing with periods you can change
  • Party statements
  • TDS receivable tracking

Compliance

GST, TDS, income-tax and ROC due dates for every client in one To Do list.

All compliance features
  • Due dates set up from each client’s work
  • To Do list: pending and overdue returns, work ready to bill, client reminders and filed returns by month
  • A calendar, and extended due dates
  • Event-based forms such as DIR-12, MGT-14 and PAS-3, due a set number of days after the event
  • Mark returns filed with the UDIN, or get a reminder when the UDIN isn’t generated yet
  • Email clients to send their documents
  • Quarterly, half-yearly and yearly bills show as ready only after every return of the period is filed
  • GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B status with due dates, and locking a month once filed
  • A branch with its own GSTIN in another state files its own returns

Certificates and letters

41 CA certificate formats, and engagement and appointment letters prepared every year from each client’s work.

All certificate and letter features
  • Net worth, turnover, Form 15CB, utilisation and end use of funds
  • Bank, RERA, NBFC, GST refund, KYC, and visa or study loan net worth
  • Others, plus your own wording
  • Client details fill in by themselves, figures and totals are worked out, with the UDIN and partner, as a PDF or email on the office’s letterhead
  • Engagement letters on your letterhead and appointment letters on the client’s:
    • Standard or Detailed wording, with terms that protect the firm
    • Fees for the year, with how often each service is billed
    • The right signatory: proprietor, partner, designated partner with DPIN, director with DIN, karta or trustee
    • Three client letterhead styles
    • “Same as last year” or “From current work”
  • Long documents split into A4 pages, with the letterhead and table headings repeated
  • Each office prepares and keeps its own

From due date to paid invoice

Compliance and billing are one flow in Shakha, so filed work never goes unbilled.

  1. Due dates appear

    GST, TDS, income-tax and ROC due dates are set up from each client’s work and show in the To Do list.

  2. Staff mark it filed

    They record the UDIN, or Shakha reminds them when it isn’t generated yet. Clients get an email when documents are needed.

  3. It shows as ready to bill

    Quarterly, half-yearly and yearly bills wait until every return of the period is filed.

  4. The invoice goes out

    A GST invoice goes by email or WhatsApp, and payment reminders follow until it’s paid.

Your firm’s data stays with your firm

Your clients, invoices and filings are kept in your own Google account, where you can open them any time. Read how it works

  • Stored in your Google Drive

    Your data sheet, invoice PDFs and backups live in a “Shakha” folder in your own Drive. Shakha can open only the files it creates there.

  • Sent from your Gmail

    Invoices, reminders and sign-in codes go from your firm’s Gmail. Shakha can send email but can’t read, search or delete it.

  • Yours if you leave

    Export your data at any time. If you stop using Shakha, your files stay in your Drive.

    Questions firms ask

    Can’t find your answer? Ask us.

    Where is our firm’s data kept?

    In your own Google Drive. Shakha creates a folder named “Shakha – your firm name” with your data sheet, and saves your invoice PDFs, statements and backups there. Your firm owns these files.

    Can Shakha read our email or our other Drive files?

    No. Shakha asks Google only for access to the files it creates, and for permission to send email. It can’t see other files in your Drive, and it can’t read, search or delete your email.

    How does the free trial work?

    Choose a plan and register with your head office admin’s Gmail. You get 15 days free with no payment needed to start. After that, you pay for the plan that fits your firm’s size.

    Can each branch have its own GSTIN, numbering and letterhead?

    Yes. Each branch has its own logins, invoice numbering, GSTIN, bank details, partners and letterhead. A branch with its own GSTIN in another state files its own GST returns.

    Does the head office see branch work?

    Yes. The head office sees and controls everything. It can work as the whole firm, as the head office only, or as any one branch. Branch staff see only their branch’s work.

    Do we need to install anything?

    No. Shakha works in any modern browser on a computer or phone. There’s also an Android app, which adds notifications, a home-screen widget and tap-to-call.

    Can we send our data to Tally?

    Yes. Shakha gives a Tally XML export, invoice and receipt registers in CSV, a GST summary and a GSTR-1 Excel workbook.

    How do staff sign in?

    With a 6-digit code sent to their email, or a password. They can also scan a QR code with their phone to sign in on a computer. You can see every signed-in device and sign any of them out.

    What happens if we stop using Shakha?

    Your files stay in your Drive. You can export your data at any time, and remove Shakha’s access from your Google Account. Email us to have your registration and logins deleted, and we’ll do it within 30 days.
