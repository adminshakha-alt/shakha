What Shakha 360 does

Six parts of a CA firm’s day, handled in one place.

Billing

GST invoices, proformas, receipts and notes, numbered for each office, sent by email or WhatsApp from your own letterhead.

Collections

Who owes what, with payment reminders that go out on their own, and receivable ageing for the whole firm.

Compliance

Every client’s GST, TDS, income-tax and ROC due dates in one To Do list. Mark a return filed, and it moves to the bill.

Client documents

Ask one client or all of them for their papers. They upload through a private link, and you see what came in.

Certificates and letters

41 CA certificate formats, and engagement and appointment letters, with the partner’s signature and the UDIN.

Reports and exports

Sales, collections, outstanding, GST summary, GSTR-1 workbook, Tally export and the UDIN register.

Every deadline, before it’s due

Each client’s returns appear in the To Do list on their own, with due dates worked out for you.

  • See what is overdue, due this week and due next month
  • Mark returns filed with the UDIN, one or many at a time
  • Filed work shows as ready to bill, so nothing goes unbilled
  • Remind clients about deposits and payments they make themselves
New

Documents come to you

Ask for the papers you need this month, from one client or from all of them, in one go.

  • Each client gets a private link by email or WhatsApp, with nothing to install
  • They upload from a phone or computer, no sign-in needed
  • Reminders go out until the documents are in
  • See who has sent what, client by client, month by month

Staff logins

Allot each client’s work to a person. They sign in and see only that work.

  • Prepared returns come to you for review
  • Each person’s pending work in one view

Head office sees the firm. Each branch runs its own.

Switch between the whole firm, head office and any branch. Branch staff see only their branch.

  • Each branch has its own logins, numbering, GSTIN, bank details and letterhead
  • A branch can keep its documents in its own Google account
  • One client list, one services list, one To Do for the firm

Your firm’s data stays with your firm

Your clients, invoices and filings are kept in your own Google account, where you can open them any time. Read how it works

  • Stored in your Google Drive

    Your data sheet, PDFs and client documents live in a folder in your own Drive. Shakha 360 can open only the files it creates there.

  • Sent from your Gmail

    Invoices, reminders and sign-in codes go from your firm’s Gmail. Shakha 360 can send email but can’t read it.

  • Yours if you leave

    Export your data at any time. If you stop using Shakha 360, your files stay in your Drive.

    Questions firms ask

    Can’t find your answer? Ask us.

    Where is our firm’s data kept?

    In your own Google Drive, in a folder your firm owns, with your data sheet, PDFs, backups and the documents clients send you.

    Can Shakha 360 read our email or our other Drive files?

    No. It can use only the files it creates, and it can send email but not read it.

    How does the free trial work?

    Choose a plan and register with your head office admin’s Gmail. You get 15 days free with no payment needed to start.

    Can each branch have its own GSTIN, numbering and letterhead?

    Yes. Each branch has its own logins, invoice numbering, GSTIN, bank details, partners and letterhead, and head office sees all of it.

    Can staff have limited access?

    Yes. A staff login shows only the work allotted to that person. Returns they prepare come to you for review.

    How do clients send us their documents?

    You send a request, and each client gets a private link. They open it on a phone or computer, with no sign-in, and upload their files. The files go straight to your Google Drive.

    Do we need to install anything?

    No. Shakha 360 works in any modern browser. There is also an Android app, with notifications and a home-screen widget.

    Can we send our data to Tally?

    Yes. Shakha 360 gives a Tally export, invoice and receipt registers, a GST summary and a GSTR-1 Excel workbook.

    What happens if we stop using Shakha 360?

    Your files stay in your Drive. Export your data at any time, and remove Shakha 360’s access from your Google Account.
