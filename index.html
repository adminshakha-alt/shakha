One firm. Every branch. Every deadline.

Shakha runs the billing and compliance of a CA firm across its head office and branches: GST invoices, receipts, collections and client due dates in one place.

Register your firm

Billing

Tax invoices, proformas, receipts, TDS, credit and debit notes, recurring retainers, PDFs by email and WhatsApp.

Collections

Outstanding and overdue lists, automatic payment reminders, receivable ageing and party statements.

Compliance

GST, TDS, income-tax and ROC due dates for every client, a To Do list, UDIN, client reminders and billing filed work.

Head office and branches

Each branch has its own login, numbering, GSTIN and bank details; head office sees and controls everything.

Your data in your Drive

Your firm's data sheet, PDFs and backups are created in your own Google Drive, and Shakha's emails go from your own Gmail.

Reports

Sales and collection reports, GST summary, GSTR-1 workbook, Tally export, registers and UDIN register.

How it works

  1. Register your firm with your head office admin's Gmail.
  2. Once approved, click Connect my Google account in the email. Shakha creates your firm's folder and data sheet in your own Google Drive.
  3. Sign in, add your branches and staff, and start billing.
