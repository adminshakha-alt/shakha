What Shakha 360 does

Everything a CA firm runs on, in one place.

Billing

GST invoices, proformas, receipts and notes for every office, on your own letterhead.

Collections

Outstanding fees, ageing and payment reminders.

Compliance

GST, TDS, income-tax and ROC due dates for every client, in one To Do list.

Work allocation

A partner and a staff member for every piece of work, with each person’s workload.

Notices and appeals

Notices, hearings, orders and appeals, with every date tracked.

Client documents

Document requests, client files and portal logins, client by client.

Certificates and letters

41 certificate formats, and engagement and appointment letters, with UDIN.

Reports and exports

Sales, collections, outstanding, GST and UDIN reports.

Tally Connector

Invoices and receipts reach TallyPrime on their own.

    Questions firms ask

    Where is our firm’s data kept?

    In your own Google Drive, in a folder your firm owns.

    How does the free trial work?

    Choose a plan and register. You get 15 days free, with no payment needed to start.

    Does it work for a firm with branches?

    Yes. Each branch has its own logins, numbering, GSTIN and letterhead, and head office sees the whole firm.

    Do we need to install anything?

    No. It works in the browser on a computer or a phone.

    Does it work with Tally?

    Yes, with the Shakha Tally Connector for TallyPrime.

    What happens if we stop using Shakha 360?

    Your files stay in your Drive.
